THE ATELIER PROCESS

Our 4-Step Bespoke Styling Journey

How Floralis transforms your wedding vision into an unforgettable botanical masterpiece.

01

Vision & Palette Discovery

We meet in our private atelier or via 3D virtual call to review your venue layout, dress details, and color palette.

02

3D Botanical Renderings

Our master florists craft 3D spatial renders and flower swatches of your ceremony arch, stage backdrop, and tablescapes.

03

Dawn Eco-Farm Harvest

Rare imported stems and garden roses are harvested at peak dawn bloom and hydrated for 24-hour flawless freshness.

04

On-Site Master Setup

Our white-glove team arrives at sunrise to construct grand arches, place candlelit runners, and ensure zero-stress execution.
