Farm-to-Table Eco Promise

Our commitment to eco-friendly cultivation, fair-trade farming, and zero plastic floral packaging.

Plastic-Free Eco Packaging

All bouquets and hat boxes use 100% biodegradable craft paper, natural jute twines, and compostable stem moisture wraps.
